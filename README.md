# 🌈🐾 Chuyu's Critter Quest: Rainbow Island RPG

🎮 **Play Live (GitHub Pages):** **[https://kunruiw1991.github.io/critters-quest-rpg/](https://kunruiw1991.github.io/critters-quest-rpg/)**

A cozy, kid-friendly **2D classic RPG** — a Pokémon-style adventure where **Chuyu** explores a huge
**120 × 174 tile world (20,880 tiles)** across **12 hand-themed lands** (Rainbow Island, **Honmoon Island** with the
**K-Pop Demon Hunters** 🎤, **Derpy's Minhwa Sanctum** 🐯, the **Nightmare Critters Carnival** 🎪, and **Kanto Classics Bay** ⚡),
makes friends with villagers & heroes, rights knocked-over flowerpots, opens treasure chests, shakes fruit trees, and
**catches all 56 Critters** for the CritterDex!

## ✨ Highlights

- **Huge 120 × 174 world (20,880 tiles, 48 px tiles)**: Rainbow Island (`rows 0..89`), Honmoon Island (`rows 90..117`), and the Southern Continent (`rows 118..173`), generated deterministically. Every chest, flowerpot, tree, NPC, fountain, crystal and rare spot is verified reachable on foot (`World.audit().unreachable` is always `[]` and `repairs === 0`).
